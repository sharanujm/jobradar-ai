🛡️ 4 Golden Rules of Tech Salary Negotiation in India:

  • Never accept on the spot: Always express excitement and ask for 48 hours to review the full compensation breakdown.
  • Base Salary is King: Benefits and ESOPs don't pay immediate monthly bills; maximize the fixed component.
  • Ask for a Sign-On Bonus: If the base pay band is locked by HR policy, ask for a one-time joining bonus (₹1L - ₹3L).
  • Get everything in writing: Never resign from your current company until the formal signed offer letter is in your inbox.
